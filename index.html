What exactly do I receive for the $350 fee?

It specifies whether the senior consultant recommends surgery, their proposed approach, the exact implant brand (e.g., Stryker or Zimmer Biomet), the locked package price, and your required inpatient and fit-to-fly recovery timeline.

Why should I pay $350 before booking flights?

Flying blind across the world is a $2,500+ financial risk. Why not use a "free" medical tourism agency?

"Free" agencies take 15% to 25% commissions paid by hospitals, meaning you are routed to whoever pays the highest kickback. MySurgicalAlly works solely for you on a transparent flat fee. You receive direct hospital rates with zero hidden markups.

How are the actual surgical fees paid?

All surgery, hospital room, and implant fees are paid by you directly to the hospital's official cashier upon admission and discharge. MySurgicalAlly never touches or marks up your hospital medical funds.
